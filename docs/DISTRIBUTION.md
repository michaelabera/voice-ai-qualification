# Distribution Playbook

The repository earns attention when someone lands on it. This playbook is how you
get them to land. Honest framing first: stars and inbound come from *distribution*,
not from the README. No single post goes viral on command. What works is showing
up consistently in the places your three audiences (recruiters, founders,
engineers) already look, with a genuinely useful artifact behind the link.

## Before you launch (the 20-minute checklist)

- [ ] Replace every `michaelabera` in the README with your real handle.
- [ ] Add repo topics on GitHub: `voice-ai`, `conversational-ai`, `lead-qualification`,
      `sales-automation`, `ai-agents`, `inbound`, `twilio`, `vapi`, `retell`.
- [ ] Write a one-line repo description and pin it to your profile.
- [ ] Record a 20–40s terminal GIF of `python src/router.py --demo` and add it
      under the "See it run" section. (Use `asciinema` + `agg`, or any screen
      recorder.) A moving demo is the single highest-converting element.
- [ ] Confirm CI is green (the badge sells credibility at a glance).

## Launch sequence (one week)

**Day 1 — LinkedIn.** Your primary audience for recruiters and founders. Post the
launch note (see `LAUNCH_POST.md`), link the repo, ask one question to drive
comments. Reply to every comment in the first 3 hours; the algorithm rewards it.

**Day 2 — Hacker News (Show HN).** Title: `Show HN: A vendor-neutral decision
architecture for inbound voice-AI agents`. Post Tue–Thu, ~9am ET. Add a top
comment explaining what's yours vs. what's standard, and the problem it solves.
Engage every reply; don't be defensive.

**Day 3 — Reddit.** r/SaaS, r/Entrepreneur, r/artificial, r/LocalLLaMA (for the
technical crowd). Lead with the problem (missed-call leads), not the repo. Link
in a comment, not the title, where subs require it.

**Day 4 — Dev communities.** dev.to or Hashnode article: "How I architect an
inbound voice-AI agent so no lead is ever a dead end." Embed the Mermaid diagrams.
Cross-link the repo.

**Day 5 — Targeted DMs.** Send the repo to 5–10 specific people: founders running
call-heavy businesses, recruiters hiring for AI/automation roles, peers in the
space. Personalized, short, no pitch beyond "built this, thought it might be
useful."

## Ongoing (the part that compounds)

- Turn each `docs/` file into a standalone post over the following weeks.
- Ship visible improvements (new vertical presets) and post each one.
- Respond to issues and PRs fast and publicly.
- Add it to your resume, portfolio, and LinkedIn featured section.

## What to measure

| Signal | Why it matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Unique repo visitors (GitHub Insights) | Real reach, not vanity stars |
| Profile views after each post | Recruiter/founder interest |
| Inbound DMs / connection requests | The actual outcome you want |
| Stars + forks | Social proof for the next visitor |

## Honest expectations

Most quality repos get tens to low-hundreds of stars over months, not thousands
overnight. The win you're after is not virality; it's the *right* 20 people
seeing proof you can architect a production AI system. One founder DM or one
recruiter conversation is worth more than 500 anonymous stars.
